How it works / CSV format

Columns: timestamp,item,price,quantity,market. ISO timestamps; prices in copper per item, quantity available at that price, market exactly eu-normal-alliance. Each timestamp represents a snapshot. Import new snapshots regularly; the deployed Worker can load live commodity history. Simulated data is kept separate from live history. Live items are initially labelled by item ID.

Signals require at least three historical snapshots spanning 24 hours within seven days, a current quote less than 24 hours old, a sufficient discount and positive estimated profit after a 5% auction cut and optional extra costs. Resale target is the prior median, not a promise. Quantity and budget caps limit exposure; recommendations are independent, not a combined shopping basket. Listing quantity is not sales volume. No in-game automation.
