Method

Screen against 7- and 30-day medians. For shortlisted items, check 7-day hourly history for unusual deviations using median absolute deviation (with a 5% noise floor), sustained falls, supply shocks and estimated turnover. Conservative resale targets are below the current and historical median prices. Profit deducts 5% AH commission and optional costs. Strong candidate is a heuristic, not a probability or a validated forecast. A low price alone is not evidence of a bargain; check price history, variant, demand, listing deposits and whether the whole stack fits your budget. Sell timing uses four-hour UK local-time bands across complete days, requiring at least four days, 8% median lift and consistency on at least 75% of days. High listing prices are not confirmed sales. Weekly seasonality and exact future peaks are not established. No actual sales or recovery are guaranteed. No in-game automation. Data is fetched on demand and not archived. Provider API and terms.
